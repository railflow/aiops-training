# 0001. Record architecture decisions

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-05

## Context

New people (and new AI agents) cannot see why the system is the way it is, and keep re-arguing settled choices.

## Decision

We will keep one short ADR per significant decision in `docs/adr/`, numbered, immutable once accepted (supersede, do not edit).

## Consequences

A small writing cost per decision, and a searchable history. Pull requests that change architecture link their ADR.
